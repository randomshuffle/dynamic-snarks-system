# Earlier Ligerito experiments

This directory separates earlier exploratory work from the final paper suite.
Each retained experiment has a short `EXPERIMENT.md` explaining its purpose.
The [result index](results/README.md) lists 36 investigations from the binary-field
Poseidon2b/Ligerito development period, with selected small summaries and plots.
The two [source-experiment notes](experiments/) describe the earlier 72-byte
Poseidon2b prototype and hash/GKR ablations; their obsolete source has been removed.

Pre-Ligerito BN254/WHIR experiments and results have been deleted, along with
historical raw logs, executable copies, checkpoint binaries, and transferred
archives. Historical summaries are retained for context and do not constitute
new final measurements.

The complete current source is in [final](../final/), and the three final
experiments and all their timing samples are in [results/paper](../results/paper/).
Follow the [reproduction guide](../docs/reproduce.md) to run them.

`local-artifacts/private-metadata/`, when present in a local checkout, is an
ignored backup of text redacted during publication preparation. It is excluded
from the public source package.
