# Historical Poseidon2b hash and GKR ablations

These exploratory benchmarks tested GKR scheduling, equivalent message kernels, checkpoint/oracle layouts and Frobenius reductions, and native Flock versus GKR reductions. They informed the final backend but are separate from the paper's sweep and update trajectories. Selected later reports and measurements are under `archive/results/`.

The standalone tuning binaries have been removed from the publication tree. The GKR implementation required by the final prover remains under `final/dynamic-ligerito/src/hash_gkr`.
